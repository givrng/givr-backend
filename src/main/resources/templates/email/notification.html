Hi Name,

How did you get into volunteering?

I wasn't one of those people who grew up volunteering every weekend. I didn't even know where to start from. I just knew that whenever I heard about an orphanage or children who needed help, I wanted to do something.

The first time I volunteered was because a friend invited me. She gathered a few of us, and we spent the day at an orphanage with snacks and clothes we'd all contributed ourselves.

Looking back, it wasn't about how much we gave. It was the feeling of knowing that showing up that day really mattered to the children.

Years later, that feeling became Givr.

And it got me thinking:

What if children didn't have to wait until they got to university or met the "right people" before they discovered the joy of giving back?

What if they started much earlier?

That's how Pillars of Tomorrow was born.

Our first program for children 🌱

Pillars of Tomorrow is Givr's very first program created specifically for children.

Over three days, they'll learn, play, ask questions, solve problems, and explore what it means to make a difference.

Most importantly, we hope they'll leave believing that making a difference isn't something they have to wait until they're older to do.

They can start cultivating the habit now.

This is a new chapter for Givr, and I'm really excited about it.

If you have a child you'd like to register, or there's a parent in your life who would love this for their child, we'd love for you to share it with them.
